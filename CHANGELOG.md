# Changelog

All notable changes to MonoTube are documented here. The format follows
[Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/), and versions follow
[Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/).

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-12

### Added

- MonoTube homepage that replaces YouTube's recommendation feed, with a single search box.
- Clean search results: shelves, Mixes, artist carousels, Shorts and promoted results removed, and infinite
  scroll replaced by a "Show more results" button.
- Distraction-free watch page: no related-videos sidebar, a centred player, a "Back to results" button and
  YouTube Mixes opened as the plain video.
- Autoplay switched off, and end screens, end cards and info cards hidden.
- Shorts blocked everywhere, with a notice offering to watch a Shorts link as a regular video.
- Ad and tracker filtering: static declarativeNetRequest rules, cosmetic filtering, and muting plus
  fast-forwarding of in-stream video ads.
- Settings popup with every feature toggle, a Dark / Light / System theme picker and "Restore original YouTube".
- Keyboard shortcuts: `/` to search, `Esc` to clear, `Alt+M` to toggle MonoTube.
